# Top 12 Overseas Web Style Atlas

Use this file as the style reference. Keep explanations practical and choice-oriented.

## 01 Modern SaaS Clean

- Feel: white or near-white base, soft color blocks, clear hierarchy, friendly professionalism.
- Layout: generous whitespace, product screenshot area, shallow card sections, restrained shadows.
- Palette: `#F8FBF9`, `#DCEEE8`, `#B8D7CC`, `#365D52`, `#18251F`.
- Best for: SaaS, AI tools, B2B products, productivity tools, consulting services.
- Caution: avoid becoming a bland template; add one distinctive product visual or typography move.

## 02 Editorial Tech

- Feel: magazine-like, intelligent, content-rich, premium.
- Layout: large serif or high-contrast headline, strict grid, editorial image treatment, small metadata.
- Palette: `#F3EFE7`, `#FFFFFF`, `#171717`, `#A76446`, `#8A8A8A`.
- Best for: AI labs, research orgs, premium professional services, founder stories.
- Caution: if the copy is weak, this style feels empty.

## 03 Linear / Vercel Dark

- Feel: technical, sharp, calm, developer-native.
- Layout: dark surface, thin lines, code panels, subtle glow, minimal nav.
- Palette: `#0E1117`, `#1F2937`, `#7AA2FF`, `#E7EEF8`, `#141925`.
- Best for: APIs, developer tools, infra, model platforms, security products.
- Caution: avoid overusing neon; keep contrast readable.

## 04 Stripe-ish Business

- Feel: colorful, commercial, platform-like, international.
- Layout: white base with vivid geometric planes, payment/data cards, confident CTA.
- Palette: `#F7F9FF`, `#6957FF`, `#20C997`, `#172033`, `#8AC5FF`.
- Best for: fintech, marketplaces, enterprise platforms, payment or commerce tools.
- Caution: gradients/geometric color need discipline; do not let them overpower content.

## 05 Notion / Figma Friendly

- Feel: approachable, smart, collaborative, low-pressure.
- Layout: simple white/off-white, playful modules, light product blocks, friendly microcopy.
- Palette: `#FBFAF7`, `#FFE066`, `#91D5FF`, `#22201D`, `#F1EEE7`.
- Best for: collaboration, notes, creators, education, small-team productivity.
- Caution: too much cuteness can weaken credibility.

## 06 Apple Premium Minimal

- Feel: premium, quiet, precise, high confidence.
- Layout: huge whitespace, one hero object, strong visual focus, very few colors.
- Palette: `#F6F7F8`, `#E2E5E8`, `#7B8087`, `#111315`, `#FFFFFF`.
- Best for: hardware, consumer apps, high-end software, flagship product pages.
- Caution: requires strong visuals; weak mockups look unfinished.

## 07 Swiss International

- Feel: rational, design-literate, structured, museum-grade.
- Layout: hard grid, large sans typography, asymmetric placement, tiny high-saturation accents.
- Palette: `#F8F8F1`, `#101010`, `#0047FF`, `#FFF200`, `#E6E3D8`.
- Best for: studios, architecture, culture, portfolios, serious design systems.
- Caution: can feel cold if copy and imagery lack personality.

## 08 Bento Grid

- Feel: modular, modern, easy to scan, feature-rich.
- Layout: large bento blocks with varied emphasis, product screenshots, metrics, capability cards.
- Palette: `#EEF4F7`, `#FFFFFF`, `#C8E6FF`, `#162736`, `#8BB8D8`.
- Best for: AI tools, multi-feature SaaS, product launches, dashboards.
- Caution: now common; use stronger content hierarchy to avoid sameness.

## 09 Neo Brutalism

- Feel: bold, loud, irreverent, young.
- Layout: thick borders, hard shadows, high-contrast color, big typography, simple shapes.
- Palette: `#FFF5C2`, `#FF4F1F`, `#111111`, `#FFFFFF`, `#00D084`.
- Best for: campaigns, creator tools, events, youth brands, experimental products.
- Caution: risky for serious B2B or regulated industries.

## 10 Luxury Editorial

- Feel: premium, slow, tactile, refined.
- Layout: serif headline, large photographic hero, understated navigation, elegant spacing, small gold/bronze accent.
- Palette: `#EEE7D9`, `#241B17`, `#B9975B`, `#FFFFFF`, `#6E5B45`.
- Best for: high-end personal brands, consulting, architecture, art, fashion, boutique services.
- Caution: avoid fake luxury clichés; keep copy restrained and visuals high quality.

## 11 Data Dashboard

- Feel: practical, information-dense, operational, trustworthy.
- Layout: filters, tables, charts, status indicators, KPI cards, navigation rails.
- Palette: `#F5F7FA`, `#E6EDF5`, `#2F80ED`, `#1C2D43`, `#8EA4BE`.
- Best for: admin panels, analytics, trading/research tools, ops systems.
- Caution: not a marketing hero by default; optimize for repeated use and scanning.

## 12 Playful Startup

- Feel: bright, warm, expressive, young.
- Layout: irregular shapes, lively colors, simple illustrations, big approachable headline.
- Palette: `#FFF7EE`, `#FF6B6B`, `#71D8C9`, `#2B2B2B`, `#FFBD59`.
- Best for: education, community, creator platforms, consumer products.
- Caution: can feel unserious; balance playfulness with clear product value.

## Mixing Rules

Use one primary style and at most one secondary style.

Useful combinations:
- Modern SaaS Clean + Bento Grid: clean SaaS page with modular feature sections.
- Editorial Tech + Swiss International: high-design thought leadership or studio page.
- Stripe-ish Business + Modern SaaS Clean: commercial platform page without old corporate feel.
- Data Dashboard + Modern SaaS Clean: enterprise utility with a more approachable shell.
- Luxury Editorial + Apple Premium Minimal: premium brand/product page with restraint.

Avoid mixing:
- Neo Brutalism + Luxury Editorial unless the user asks for intentional tension.
- Playful Startup + Corporate/Data Dashboard for serious finance or enterprise work.
- More than two styles in one page.
